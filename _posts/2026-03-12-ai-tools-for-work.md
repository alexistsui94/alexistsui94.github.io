AI tools have become part of my daily workflow.

I mainly use them in four ways:

1. Drafting and refining written materials
2. Supporting coding and debugging
3. Organizing meeting notes and project documentation
4. Accelerating repetitive knowledge work

Over time, I have found that the value of AI is not only in speed, but also in helping structure thoughts more clearly.
