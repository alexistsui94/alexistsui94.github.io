This post reviews a project from the perspective of delivery and execution.

The process can usually be broken into several stages:

1. Requirement clarification
2. Blueprint and solution design
3. System configuration and testing
4. Go-live preparation
5. Post-launch stabilization

What matters most is not only technical completion, but alignment between business needs, system logic, and delivery control.
