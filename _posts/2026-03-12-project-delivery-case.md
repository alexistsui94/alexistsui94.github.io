<h2>What I would check next time<span class="h-zh" lang="zh-Hans">下次我会先检查什么</span></h2>

<div class="seg">
<p class="en">When a stage / feels “done”, / I ask one question: / can the next stage / pick up a concrete output / without a verbal explanation? // If the answer is no, / the stage / is not finished yet. //</p>
<p class="zh" lang="zh-Hans">当某个阶段看起来「做完了」，我会问一个问题：下一阶段能不能拿到一份具体产出，而不需要口头补充？如果答案是否定的，这个阶段就还没完成。</p>
</div>

<div class="seg">
<p class="en">That single check / catches / most handover problems / before they become / rework later. //</p>
<p class="zh" lang="zh-Hans">这一条检查，能在大多数交接问题演变成后期返工之前就把它们找出来。</p>
</div>
